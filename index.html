Create a QR code that scans reliably

A QR code converts text or structured data into a square pattern that phone cameras can read. This QR code generator creates static codes: the destination is stored directly in the pattern, with no tracking redirect, embedded advertisement or expiration date.

For reliable scanning, use a dark QR color on a light background, keep the clear margin around the code and test the final image at its intended print size. When adding a logo, use High (H) error correction and avoid covering the three large corner markers.

Popular QR code uses

  • URL QR code: open a website, menu, form or social profile.
  • WiFi QR code: let guests join a network without typing the password.
  • vCard QR code: save a name, phone number and email as a contact.
  • QR code with logo: create a branded code for packaging, signs or business cards.

QR code generator FAQ

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. You can create and download unlimited static QR codes without an account, watermark or paid plan.

Do QR codes created here expire?

No. The generator creates static QR codes that store content directly in the pattern, so they do not depend on a paid redirect or subscription.

Can I add a logo to a QR code?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG logo. The generator automatically selects high error correction to improve scan reliability.

Is my QR code content uploaded?

The QR generator processes the content locally in your browser and does not upload the content for QR creation.

What do “QR gen” and other QR search terms mean?

QR gen is informal shorthand for a QR code generator. Searches such as creator qr code, qr kód generator and qr code generátor are language or word-order variations for tools that create a scannable QR code.
