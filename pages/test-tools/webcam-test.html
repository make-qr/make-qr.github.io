How to test your webcam

  1. Click Start Webcam Test.
  2. Allow camera permission when your browser asks.
  3. Check the preview, resolution, and selected camera.
  4. Use mirror mode or take a snapshot if needed.

Common webcam problems

  • Permission denied: reopen browser camera permissions and allow access.
  • Black screen: close Zoom, Meet, Teams, or apps using the camera.
  • No camera found: reconnect external webcam or check device drivers.
  • Blurry image: clean the lens and improve room lighting.

Webcam Test FAQ

Is this webcam test private?

Yes. The camera preview runs locally in your browser. No video is uploaded to a server.

Do you record my webcam?

No. This tool only displays your webcam stream so you can test it.

Can I test an external webcam?

Yes. Connect the webcam, start the test, then select it from the camera dropdown.

Why is my webcam not working?

The most common causes are denied permissions, another app using the camera, disconnected hardware, or outdated drivers.
